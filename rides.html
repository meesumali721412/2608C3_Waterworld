🛡️ Ride Safety & Water Park Rules

At Sanjalika Water Park, safety is our paramount priority. All slides undergo certified daily robotic ultrasound thickness checks and certified morning test runs.

  • Follow the instructions of certified lifeguards posted at each slide tower and splash landing zone.
  • Proper 100% lycra/nylon swimwear with no metal zippers, buckles, or sharp buttons is strictly mandatory.
  • Pregnant guests and guests with serious neck/back ailments or cardiac conditions must avoid extreme rides.
  • Life vests are provided free of charge at all swimming pools and the Tsunami Wave Pool.
