Visitor Guidelines

Dress Code & Park Etiquette

To maintain pristine water hygiene in our state-of-the-art pools and protect fiberglass slides, please review our swimwear guidelines.

Strictly Permitted Swimwear

  • 100% Lycra or Synthetic Nylon Swimsuits & Bikinis.
  • Synthetic board shorts (without rivets or metal buttons).
  • Full body UV swim rash guards & burkinis (pure nylon).
  • Water diapers for infants & toddlers (available at park shop).
  • Costumes can be rented or purchased at our on-site locker counters.

Strictly Prohibited Clothing & Items

  • Cotton clothing, jeans, denim shorts, cargo pants, or sarees.
  • Any garment with metal buckles, zippers, or chains.
  • Outside food and beverages (baby milk permitted).
  • Glass bottles, sharp items, alcoholic beverages, or tobacco.
  • Shoes or hard slippers in pools or ride queue areas.
Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to view immediate answers about parking, lockers, rides, and discounts.

What is included with my general admission Splash Pass?
Your admission ticket includes unlimited all-day rides on all 20+ thrill slides, access to the Great Mystical Cave River, the Aqua Tsunami Wave Pool, Olympic swimming pool, children's Mermaid Fortress, and all mermaid theme photo spots. Food and locker rentals are available as optional add-ons.
How does the RFID Locker rental system work?
Upon entering the locker area, you receive a waterproof RFID wristband. Simply tap your wristband on the digital scanner assigned to your locker to lock and unlock it at any time during your visit. You can also load digital cash onto the wristband for contactless payments at our Food Zone!
Are there height and weight limits on the extreme water slides?
Yes, for guest safety: The Mermaid's Abyss and Boomerango require a minimum height of 120 cm (approx 4ft). Poseidon's Thunder Drop freefall requires 130 cm. Gentle family slides, the Lazy Cave River, and Mermaid Fortress accommodate smaller children and guests of all heights.
Is parking available at Sanjalika Water Park?
Yes! We have an expansive, 1,500-vehicle capacity secure shaded parking lot for cars, two-wheelers, and tourist buses. Dedicated EV charging stations and disability parking spots are available right near the main entrance turnstiles.
Can I book my tickets in advance online?
Absolutely! Booking online through our website gives you direct turnstile entry with zero waiting at the ticket booth, plus exclusive seasonal coupon discounts such as 20% off with promo code MERMAID20.
