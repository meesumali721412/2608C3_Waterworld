## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges modern aquatic spectacle, mythological allure, and extreme recreational energy. It caters to thrill-seeking youth, vacationing families, and luxury resort-goers. The emotional goal is to evoke instant refreshment, exhilaration, and luminous wonder—reminiscent of sunlight penetrating clear ocean water and glowing cave lagoons.

The visual style blends **Contemporary Refreshing Aquatic** and **Subtle Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-clean, luminous surfaces with dynamic water-sheen depth.
- Tactile, rounded structural cards that reflect the organic motion of waves and slides.
- Translucent frosted overlays resembling ocean spray and pool surfaces.
- High-energy energetic pops of warm sunset and electric coral to signify high thrills, urgent booking states, and primary interaction triggers.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high aquatic saturation grounded by oceanic deep navy:

- **Primary (`#00A9E0` - Electric Azure):** Drives the primary branding, fluid water indicators, active states, and ambient wave effects.
- **Secondary (`#FF5E36` - Thrill Coral):** High-priority call-to-actions, extreme thrill ride identifiers, express lane tags, and booking conversions.
- **Tertiary (`#06D6A0` - Lagoon Mint):** Signifies open slide status, family zones, calm waters, and positive availability.
- **Neutral (`#0B192C` - Deep Oceanic Navy):** High-contrast typography, footer backdrops, immersive night-mode overlays, and structural boundaries.

### Supporting Canvas Tones
- **Canvas Base:** `#F0F8FF` (Crisp Ice Blue) for daylight luminosity without harsh glare.
- **Surface Elevation:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Water Surface).
- **Surface Frosted:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` for badges, floating navigation, and quick-filter chips.
- **Caution / Flare:** `#FFB703` (Golden Sunbeam) for heat warnings, locker notifications, and family cabana badges.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all hierarchy tiers. Its geometric clarity, wide aperture, and modern curved stroke terminals balance legibility with an energetic, friendly, and premium resort aesthetic.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold and extrabold weights with tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to give ride titles, cave attractions, and promotional headers high impact.
- **Body:** Set at regular weight with generous line spacing to ensure frictionless legibility on outdoor mobile devices under direct sunlight.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Enhanced with uppercase or high-weight medium tracking for wait times, slide intensity ratings, and live park status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system uses a flexible 12-column responsive grid on desktop, scaling to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fixed maximum container width of `1360px`, `3rem` canvas margins, and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Fluid layout with `2rem` margins, enabling horizontal scroll trays for ride galleries and food zone cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Sticky bottom conversion bar for instant ticket booking.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt-derived rhythm (`4px`, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `40px`) strictly allocated for content airiness, preventing visual clutter in complex scheduling and ticketing widgets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth mirrors the refractive transparency and layered depth of illuminated water:

- **Subsurface (Base):** Flat `#F0F8FF` canvas tone.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Ride Tiles, Food Panels):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a dual-layer soft ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 169, 224, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Widgets, Flyouts):** Lifted card state: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 169, 224, 0.16)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Booking Drawers, Critical Alerts):** Elevated overlays: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.25)`.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Sheen:** Used for header bars, live wait-time pills, and mermaid cave tags. Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy uses a modern, rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`) that evokes pool contours, eroded cavern stone, and water droplets:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `1rem` (16px) radius for soft, approachable physical edges.
- **Hero Banners & Immersive Feature Shells:** `1.5rem` (24px) radius.
- **Pill Elements (Chips, Status Pills, CTAs):** Fully rounded ends (`9999px`) to emphasize fluidity and touch friendliness.
- **Inputs & Dropdowns:** `0.75rem` (12px) radius for crisp operational clarity without feeling boxed or rigid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Thrill CTA):** Background in secondary Coral (`#FF5E36`), text white, fully rounded pill shape (`9999px`), bold weight typography. Soft coral glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(255, 94, 54, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Water Active):** Electric Azure background (`#00A9E0`), text white, pill-shaped.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Border `1.5px solid rgba(0, 169, 224, 0.3)`, text Deep Oceanic Navy (`#0B192C`), transparent background shifting to `rgba(0, 169, 224, 0.08)` on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- **Glassmorphic Status Badges:** Translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), blurred backdrop, subtle border, containing pulse indicators (e.g., green dot for "Cave Pool: 5m Wait", coral flame for "Extreme Drop: Thrill Level 5").
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped, light azure surface (`rgba(0, 169, 224, 0.08)`), text `#0B192C`. Active state switches to solid `#00A9E0` with white text.

### Cards
- **Attraction Cards:** White background, `1rem` radius, subtle water-tinted drop shadow. Media header with high-aspect photography, topped with glass tags for intensity and wait times. Content area includes ride distance, min-height specs, and fast-pass accessibility icon.
- **Food Zone & Cabana Cards:** Split layout with thumbnail, menu tags, quick pre-order button, and sun/shade indices.

### Input Fields & Booking Widgets
- **Date, Pass & Guest Selectors:** High-contrast white fields with `0.75rem` radius, bordered with `1.5px solid rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.1)`. Focus state features an Electric Azure outline (`#00A9E0`) with `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 169, 224, 0.15)`.
- **Park Timings Indicator:** Floating glass header widget displaying today's open hours, live cave wave schedule, and weather forecast icon.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squircle checkboxes (`6px` radius) and circular radio buttons. Inactive border `#0B192C` at 20% opacity. Selected state filled with `#00A9E0` featuring crisp white SVG checkmarks and inner dots.
