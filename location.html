Transit Guides

Getting Here by Any Mode of Transport

Choose the most convenient route whether you are driving, taking the metro, or riding public buses.

🚗

By Car / Private Cab

Take Exit 14 on the Coastal Expressway and follow the green "Sanjalika Water Park" overhead signs for 3.5 km. Smooth 4-lane wide asphalt road leading straight to our vehicle parking plaza.

🚇

By Metro & Free Shuttle

Alight at Ocean Bay Central Metro Station (Blue Line, Gate 3). Our complimentary open-air Sanjalika Shuttles depart every 15 minutes directly to our park entry turnstiles.

🚌

By City Bus

Buses numbered 102, 204, and AC Express 88 stop directly at the Sanjalika Water Park Main Terminus. Bus frequency is every 10 minutes from all major city junctions.

✈️

From Airport & Railway

Located just 22 km (approx 35 minutes) from the International Airport and 14 km from Central Railway Station. Pre-paid taxi and app cab drop-off bays are stationed right at Gate 1.
