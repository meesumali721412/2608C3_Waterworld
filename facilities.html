World-Class Guest Facilities

Engineered for peace of mind, premium comfort, top-tier hygiene, and seamless visitor support.

RFID Smart Lockers

More than 2,000 automated electronic lockers secured with waterproof RFID wristbands. Never worry about losing keys or handling cash while enjoying the wave pool.

Rate: PKR 400 / day
🚿

Showers & Changing Suites

Pristine gender-segregated shower suites featuring hot water geysers, hair dryers, vanity mirrors, and dedicated private family changing rooms.

Complimentary with all passes
🩱

Swimwear Rentals & Shop

Forgot your swimwear? Rent sanitized, sterilized 100% lycra swimwear or buy stylish UV-protective rash guards, goggles, and swim caps at our retail store.

Rentals from PKR 500
🚑

Medical Center & First Aid

On-site trauma center staffed 24/7 with certified doctors, registered emergency nurses, and standby ambulance facilities for immediate medical attention.

Immediate Emergency Support
⛱️

VIP Luxury Cabanas

Private, shaded poolside gazebos equipped with plush lounge beds, ceiling fans, chilled mini-coolers, charging sockets, and personal butler service.

Included in VIP Pass or PKR 4,000/day
🚗

Shaded Parking & EV Stations

Safe, CCTV-monitored parking for 1,500 vehicles and 50 tourist buses, plus fast DC Electric Vehicle charging points powered by solar panels.

Free Parking for All Guests
♿

Wheelchair & Accessibility

100% barrier-free wheelchair ramps throughout the park, aquatic pool wheelchairs, zero-depth wave pool beach entry, and dedicated accessible restrooms.

Complimentary Wheelchair Rental
🍼

Baby Care & Nursing Stations

Quiet, air-conditioned nursing pods with infant changing tables, bottle warming stations, comfortable feeding armchairs, and complimentary baby wipes.

Available at Guest Center
🦺

Free Life Jackets & Tubes

Over 3,000 ISO-certified life jackets for infants, children, and adults, along with single and double buoyant inflatable tubes for the Mystical Cave River.

Free Unlimited Usage
