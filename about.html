About Sanjalika Water Park

An ultimate, out-of-the-world source of entertainment where mythical mermaid themes meet modern hydraulic wonder.

Our Mythical Heritage & Vision

The Ultimate & Out of the World Source of Entertainment

Sanjalika Water Park is definitely an ultimate and out of the world source of entertainment. Decorated by handcrafted mermaids and captivating statues of ancient aquatic lore, the park represents a harmonious blend of mythical artistic charm and next-generation water park technology.

Sanjalika Water park is home to the largest cave and swimming pool area of modern technology — a sprawling subterranean paradise featuring stalactite formations, ambient fiber-optic mermaid illumination, crystal warm lagoons, and continuous gentle river currents engineered for supreme relaxation and thrill.

Designed for Thrills and Fun

At Sanjalika Water Park there is a lot of water rides which are very exciting and thrilling, paired with the state's largest modern cave and swimming pools.

🧜‍♀️

Mermaid Art & Statues

More than 40 life-sized and giant mermaid sculptures, coral reefs, Poseidon statues, and seashell fountains sculpt the visual landscape, creating picturesque spots across every corner of the park.

🪨

The Grandest Modern Cave

Our temperature-regulated subterranean cavern network stretches over 1,200 meters, equipped with hidden waterfall curtains, acoustic echo chambers, underwater mood lights, and smooth slow-drift currents.

💧

Modern Pool Engineering

We use multi-stage German quartz-sand and ozone purifiers that refresh 100% of our millions of liters of pool water every 90 minutes with zero harsh chemical odors, keeping skin and eyes completely safe.

🛡️

Safety & Lifeguard Protocol

Our park is certified to international ASTM aquatic amusement standards. A team of over 50 ILS-certified lifeguards patrol every pool, tower, and flume continuously throughout the day.

Our Promise to Every Visitor

Unforgettable Joy, Pristine Cleanliness & Maximum Safety

Whether you are plunging down our 80-foot freefall slide, floating through our glowing mermaid cavern, splashing in the Tsunami wave pool with your kids, or indulging in our Food Zone delicacies, Sanjalika delivers memories that last a lifetime.
